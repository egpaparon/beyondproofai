What happens next

Someone from the firm will review what you sent and call you back. If we need anything further to give you a useful answer, we will tell you exactly what.

Nothing you sent through that form creates an attorney–client relationship, and you have not committed to anything. If you would rather not wait, call (918) 583-0121 — that is always faster.

In the meantime

  • Do not give a recorded statement to an insurer.
  • Do not sign a broad medical release or a settlement.
  • Write down the timeline while you still remember it — dates, names, what was said.
  • Keep going to your medical appointments, and follow the restrictions you were given.
